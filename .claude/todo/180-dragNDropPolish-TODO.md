---- Session 2 (Opus 4.8, 2026-09-12) — the touch scroll bug from the manual test above was still present after commit `9110007`.

**Root cause (why 9110007's mount-time non-passive listener wasn't enough):**
1. `touch-action: none` was applied only *while dragging* (`TodoItem.svelte` inline style, gated on `drag.dragging`). The browser locks scroll-vs-drag in at `touchstart`; flipping `touch-action` 180 ms later, mid-gesture, does nothing for the in-flight touch.
2. `handleTouchMove` only called `preventDefault()` *after* activation. During the 180 ms hold the finger is never perfectly still — a few px of drift produce touchmoves that were not prevented. With `touch-action: auto` the compositor starts panning from that drift, later touchmoves arrive `cancelable=false`, Chrome fires `pointercancel` → the drag tears down and the board scrolls while the card snaps back. Exactly the cascade the plan warned about; the mount-time listener alone doesn't stop it because the *pending-phase* moves weren't being prevented.

**Fix (in `cardDrag.svelte.ts`, no public API change):** make the mount-time non-passive `touchmove` listener authoritative for the whole touch lifecycle:
- Pending phase: swallow (`preventDefault`) sub-tolerance moves so the compositor can never start a pan from finger drift → touchmoves after the hold stay cancelable and the drag takes over. Once travel exceeds `TOUCH_MOVE_TOLERANCE` we abandon the pending drag and stop preventing, handing the gesture back to native scroll (only the first ≤14 px is briefly held — imperceptible).
- Activated phase: always `preventDefault`, and drive the card offset straight from the touch point so the visual no longer depends on `pointermove` firing.
- Ignore multi-touch (`touches.length !== 1`) in the pending phase so pinch-zoom still works.
- Touch scroll-vs-drag arbitration moved out of `handlePointerMove` (a `pointermove` can't cancel a pan anyway); mouse keeps its distance-threshold path there.

Deliberately NOT adding a persistent `touch-action: none` on the card (would kill scroll-over-card, the whole point of the long-press) and NOT swapping in a DnD library.
